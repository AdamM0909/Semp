The story

From recruiter to the showroom floor.

As a Marine Corps veteran and former recruiter in the Hinesville and Fort Stewart area, I have dedicated my career to serving the military community.

Today, I bring that same commitment to helping service members, veterans, and their families find the right vehicle without pressure, games, or surprises.

Whether you are buying your first vehicle, upgrading for your growing family, or looking for a dependable truck, my mission remains the same.
